# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a constructive community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other people.
- Giving and accepting actionable technical feedback.
- Focusing discussion on the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct issues privately through GitHub Security Advisories:

https://github.com/Xichun123/vpsctl/security/advisories/new

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
